Backward differentiation for ddmap function. 

This subroutine computes the backward differentiation (adjoint) of the ddmap function, handling both the coordinate and cell period adjoints.


\begin{DoxyParams}[1]{Parameters}
\mbox{\texttt{in}}  & {\em x} & Input coordinate \\
\hline
\mbox{\texttt{in,out}}  & {\em xb} & Adjoint variable for the input coordinate \\
\hline
\mbox{\texttt{in}}  & {\em cell\+\_\+period} & Period of the cell in this direction \\
\hline
\mbox{\texttt{in,out}}  & {\em cell\+\_\+periodb} & Adjoint variable for the cell period \\
\hline
\mbox{\texttt{in,out}}  & {\em ddmapb} & Adjoint variable for the ddmap function output\\
\hline
\end{DoxyParams}
The backward differentiation handles the chain rule for the second derivative function\+: ddmap(x, cell\+\_\+period) = ddmap0(x/cell\+\_\+period) / cell\+\_\+period

For cell\+\_\+period ≠ 0\+:
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item cell\+\_\+periodb -\/= ddmap0(x/cell\+\_\+period) \texorpdfstring{$\ast$}{*} ddmapb / cell\+\_\+period²
\item xb += ddmap0\+\_\+b(x/cell\+\_\+period) / cell\+\_\+period
\item cell\+\_\+periodb -\/= ddmap0\+\_\+b(x/cell\+\_\+period) \texorpdfstring{$\ast$}{*} x / cell\+\_\+period²
\end{DoxyItemize}

\begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
This subroutine is used in automatic differentiation for Hessian calculations involving second derivatives of periodic mapping functions. 
\end{DoxyNote}
