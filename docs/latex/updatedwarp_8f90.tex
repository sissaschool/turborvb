Update coordinate warping and calculate forces for quantum Monte Carlo. 

This subroutine performs coordinate warping calculations and updates forces for quantum Monte Carlo simulations. It handles both isolated and periodic boundary conditions, with special treatment for electron-\/ion and ion-\/ion interactions using warping functions.\hypertarget{updatedwarp_8f90_autotoc_md62}{}\doxysubsubsection{\texorpdfstring{Parameters}{Parameters}}\label{updatedwarp_8f90_autotoc_md62}

RION \+: real\texorpdfstring{$\ast$}{*}8 array, in Ion positions (3 × NION). RMU \+: real\texorpdfstring{$\ast$}{*}8 array, inout Relative position vectors and their derivatives.
