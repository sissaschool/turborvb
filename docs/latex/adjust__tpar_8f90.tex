Automatically adjust learning rate (tpar) during optimization. 

This subroutine automatically adjusts the learning rate (tpar) during optimization based on the stability of energy and error measurements. It monitors running averages and standard deviations of energy and error, and increases or decreases tpar accordingly to maintain stable optimization. The algorithm includes mechanisms to handle unstable behavior and maintain a list of stable tpar values.
