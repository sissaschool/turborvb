\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Evaluate orbital invariants for Jastrow or symmetry-\/adapted wave functions. 

This file provides a subroutine to compute invariants (such as s-\/s, s-\/p, p-\/p) between two orbitals, which are used in the construction of Jastrow factors or symmetry-\/adapted wave functions in quantum Monte Carlo calculations. The invariants are determined based on the types of the two orbitals and their relative position vector.

subroutine evaluate\+\_\+invariant (\begin{DoxyParamCaption}\item[{real(8), dimension(\texorpdfstring{$\ast$}{*})}]{vec\+\_\+r}{, }\item[{integer}]{ix}{, }\item[{integer}]{iy}{, }\item[{integer, dimension(\texorpdfstring{$\ast$}{*})}]{typeorb}{, }\item[{real(8), dimension(4)}]{jas\+\_\+invariant}{, }\item[{logical}]{orbps}{}\end{DoxyParamCaption})}



Evaluate invariants between two orbitals for Jastrow or symmetry purposes. 

This subroutine computes a set of invariants (e.\+g., s-\/s, s-\/p, p-\/p) between two orbitals based on their types and the relative position vector. These invariants are used in the construction of Jastrow factors or symmetry-\/adapted wave functions.


\begin{DoxyParams}[1]{Parameters}
\mbox{\texttt{in}}  & {\em vec\+\_\+r} & Relative position vector between the two orbitals (length \texorpdfstring{$>$}{>}= 3) \\
\hline
\mbox{\texttt{in}}  & {\em ix} & Index of the first orbital \\
\hline
\mbox{\texttt{in}}  & {\em iy} & Index of the second orbital \\
\hline
\mbox{\texttt{in}}  & {\em typeorb} & Array specifying the type of each orbital (0=s, 1-\/3=p\+\_\+x/p\+\_\+y/p\+\_\+z) \\
\hline
\mbox{\texttt{out}}  & {\em jas\+\_\+invariant} & Array of computed invariants (length 4) \\
\hline
\mbox{\texttt{out}}  & {\em orbps} & Logical flag, true if the order of orbitals is s-\/p (for symmetry)\\
\hline
\end{DoxyParams}
The invariants are\+:
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item jas\+\_\+invariant(1)\+: s-\/s overlap (always 1 if both are s)
\item jas\+\_\+invariant(2)\+: s-\/p or p-\/s overlap (component of vec\+\_\+r)
\item jas\+\_\+invariant(3)\+: p-\/p overlap (product of components)
\item jas\+\_\+invariant(4)\+: 1 if both p orbitals are the same, 0 otherwise
\end{DoxyItemize}

\begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
The routine is designed for use in QMC codes where orbital symmetry and Jastrow factor construction require such invariants.
\end{DoxyNote}
