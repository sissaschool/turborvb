Perform hopping moves for quantum Monte Carlo with periodic boundary conditions. 

This subroutine implements a hopping algorithm for quantum Monte Carlo simulations where particles can move between different ion positions. With probability \texorpdfstring{$\vert$}{|}tjasder\texorpdfstring{$\vert$}{|}, the particle performs a hopping move to a randomly selected ion position (different from the current minimum position). With probability 1-\/\texorpdfstring{$\vert$}{|}tjasder\texorpdfstring{$\vert$}{|}, the particle performs a regular displacement move. The hopping move satisfies detailed balance by considering both forward and backward hopping directions with equal probability. The subroutine also handles periodic boundary conditions for the simulation box.
