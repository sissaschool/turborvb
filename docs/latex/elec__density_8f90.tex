subroutine cutdens}



Integrates charge and spin density over the mesh. 

This subroutine integrates the charge and spin density over the whole mesh and computes the total charge. It is called at the end of each DFT cycle to check if eigenvectors are correct by verifying that the integral of the density equals the number of electrons.

subroutine updenorb\+\_\+new}



Updates electron density and spin density on the grid. 

This subroutine computes charge (dent) and spin (spint) density on the mesh from occupations of DFT orbitals and k-\/points weights. It handles both real and complex algorithms, supports LSDA calculations, and includes double mesh capabilities for improved accuracy around atomic positions.

Uploads density contributions from grid points. 

This subroutine processes grid points and computes density contributions by evaluating wave functions and applying occupation numbers. It handles both real and complex wavefunctions, supports LSDA calculations, and includes parallel processing with memory optimization options.
