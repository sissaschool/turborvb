Sorts molecular orbitals for complex wavefunctions based on overlap. 

This subroutine sorts down-\/spin molecular orbitals according to their overlap with up-\/spin orbitals for complex wavefunctions. It handles complex conjugation and ensures proper pairing between up and down orbitals.
