Compute regularized wave function ratio. 

Computes the ratio of regularized wave functions (wfnew/wfnewr)/(wfold/wfoldr) where wf\texorpdfstring{$\ast$}{*}r means the regularized wave function. The sign does not play any role since Sign wf\texorpdfstring{$\ast$}{*}r = Sign wf\texorpdfstring{$\ast$}{*}. Different regularization types are supported. 
\begin{DoxyParams}[1]{Parameters}

\hline

\hline
\mbox{\texttt{in}}  & {\em ratio} & Ratio of new to old wave function\+: wfnew/wfold \\
\hline
\mbox{\texttt{out}}  & {\em tabler} & Regularized ratio\+: (wfnew/wfnewr)/(wfold/wfoldr) \\
\hline
\mbox{\texttt{in}}  & {\em typereg} & Type of regularization\+:
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item 2\+: Hard cutoff regularization
\item 4\+: Soft regularization with sqrt(wf\texorpdfstring{$^\wedge$}{\string^}2 + epsilon\texorpdfstring{$^\wedge$}{\string^}2)
\item 8\+: Sign regularization (wfr = sgn(wf))
\item Other\+: Standard cutoff regularization 
\end{DoxyItemize}\\
