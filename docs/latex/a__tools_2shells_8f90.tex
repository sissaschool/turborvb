Computes vectors within a cutoff radius in reciprocal space. 

Computes vectors x(ndim)=(a(1)\texorpdfstring{$\ast$}{*}n(1),...,a(ndim)\texorpdfstring{$\ast$}{*}n(ndim)) where n(i) are integers and x(1)\texorpdfstring{$^\wedge$}{\string^}2 + ... + x(ndim)\texorpdfstring{$^\wedge$}{\string^}2 \texorpdfstring{$<$}{<}= cut\texorpdfstring{$^\wedge$}{\string^}2. The vectors are stored in rkcomp ordered by their norms. Also computes the number of different norm values (nshlls) and stores the norms in rknorm with their multiplicities in kmult.
