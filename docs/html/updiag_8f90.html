Compute local energy with diagonal and off-diagonal parts for real wave functions.

Computes the local energy including both diagonal and off-diagonal parts. Handles regularization of the Coulomb potential and implements various cutoff schemes for DMC calculations. Supports different regularization types and handles nodal surface crossings.
