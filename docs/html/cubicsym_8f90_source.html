19!> @brief Generate symmetry operations for cubic and lower-symmetry Bravais lattices
20!> @details This subroutine provides symmetry operations for all cubic and lower-symmetry
21!> Bravais lattices (Hexagonal and Trigonal excepted). It generates rotation
22!> matrices and their names for crystallographic symmetry operations.
23!>
24!> The subroutine works by:
25!> 1. Computing the overlap matrix between direct lattice vectors
26!> 2. Testing each of 24 possible symmetry operations
27!> 3. Checking if the operation preserves the lattice structure
28!> 4. Adding inversion symmetry (all Bravais lattices have inversion)
29!>
30!> The symmetry operations include:
31!> - Identity and inversion
32!> - 90°, 120°, and 180° rotations around various axes
33!> - Combinations of rotations and inversions
34!>
35!> @param[in] at Real*8(3,3) Direct lattice vectors matrix
36!> @param[out] is Integer(3,3,48) Symmetry matrices in crystal coordinates
37!> @param[out] isname Character(48)*45 Names of symmetry operations
38!> @param[out] nrot Integer Number of symmetry operations found
