Apply complex constraints for AGP and Pfaffian wave functions.

This subroutine applies complex constraints for AGP (Antisymmetrized Geminal Power) and Pfaffian wave functions. It handles both real and complex parameters with proper Hermitian relations and Cauchy relations. The subroutine manages symmetry constraints for AGP matrices and handles ghost variables for Pfaffian calculations.
