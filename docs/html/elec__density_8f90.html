subroutine cutdens

Integrates charge and spin density over the mesh.

This subroutine integrates the charge and spin density over the whole mesh and computes the total charge. It is called at the end of each DFT cycle to check if eigenvectors are correct by verifying that the integral of the density equals the number of electrons.
