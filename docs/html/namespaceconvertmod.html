subroutine convertmod::convertmol_fast

Converts molecular orbitals in the wavefunction.

This subroutine appends or replaces molecular orbitals at the end of fort.10 (the wavefunction in TurboRVB notation). It handles coefficients of both molecular and atomic contracted orbitals.

Parameters

  • molopt=+/-1: Ignore coefficients of contracted orbitals

  • molopt >= 2: Do not optimize coefficients
  • molopt < 2: Always optimize contracted orbital coefficients
