Update potential energy with periodic boundary conditions and exponential Jastrow.

Computes potential energy contributions for electron-electron interactions with various Jastrow factor types. Supports periodic boundary conditions and handles different Jastrow parameterizations (iesd types). Implements exponential and rational Jastrow functions with spin-dependent interactions.
