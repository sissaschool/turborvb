Detailed Description

External forces calculation module for TurboRVB.

This module provides comprehensive force calculations for QMC/MM (Quantum Monte Carlo/Molecular Mechanics) simulations, including external potential forces, van der Waals forces, and molecular mechanics restraints.

Author
E. Coccia (4/1/11)
Date
2022

Features

  • 3D spline interpolation of external potentials
  • Force evaluation from interpolated potentials
  • Van der Waals force calculations
  • Molecular mechanics restraints (bonds, angles, dihedrals)
  • Force capping and validation
  • QM/MM link atom forces

Subroutines

Main subroutines:

  • forces_interpolate: 3D spline interpolation setup
  • forces_evaluate: Force evaluation from splines
  • ext_force: External potential forces
  • vdw_force: Van der Waals forces
  • force_capping: Force magnitude limiting
  • Molecular mechanics: force_angle, force_dihed, force_improper

Calculate forces due to external potential.

Evaluates forces on ions and electrons from the external potential using interpolated spline derivatives. Handles both nuclear and electronic forces with proper scaling.

Parameters
[in]nionNumber of ions
[in]rionIon positions (3, nion)
[in]zetarIon charges
[in]nelNumber of electrons
[in]relElectron positions (3, nel)
Note
Nuclear forces are scaled by ion charges (zetar)
Electronic forces have opposite sign (negative gradient)
Forces are zero outside the potential box boundaries
Uses forces_evaluate for derivative calculation
Handles boundary conditions for potential box

◆ force_angle()

subroutine force_angle

Calculate angle forces for QM/MM link atoms.

Computes forces due to bond angle restraints in QM/MM simulations. Handles mixed QM/MM angle interactions.

Note
Uses harmonic potential: V(theta) = k_theta * (theta - theta_eq)^2
Forces are computed for QM atoms only
Handles I-J-K angle geometry (I and K atoms, J central atom)
Accumulates forces in mm_f_theta array
Follows GROMOS force field conventions

Calculate van der Waals forces between QM and MM atoms.

Forces are accumulated in force_vdw array
Handles both QM->MM and MM->QM force calculations
