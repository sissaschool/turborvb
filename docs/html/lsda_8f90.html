Computes Perdew-Zunger correlation for fully polarized electron gas.

This subroutine calculates the correlation energy and potential for a fully polarized (spin-up only) electron gas using the Perdew-Zunger parameterization. Based on quantum Monte Carlo data.

Parameters
[in]rsWigner-Seitz radius parameter

[out]vcCorrelation potential
