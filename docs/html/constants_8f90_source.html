16!> @brief Physical and mathematical constants for quantum Monte Carlo calculations
17!>
18!> This module defines all physical constants, mathematical constants, and unit
19!> conversion factors used throughout the TurboRVB codebase for quantum Monte Carlo
20!> calculations in atomic and molecular systems.
21!>
22!> @note About measurement units: The program works internally with atomic units:
23!> - Length → Bohr radius (= 0.52917 Å)
24!> - Energy → Hartree (= 27.211383 eV)
25!> - Mass → Electron mass (9.10938291×10⁻³¹ kg)
26!> - Unit mass = 1/12 C₁₂ (1.660538921×10⁻²⁷ kg)
27!> - Time → 2.4188843×10⁻¹⁷ s
28!>
29!> Input/output units are:
30!> - Length → Ångström
31!> - Energy → eV
32!> - Mass → amu (= 1/12 Carbon mass)
33!> - Time → fs (10⁻¹⁵ s)
34!> - Pressure → kbar
35!>
